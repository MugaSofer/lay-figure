Spike C: one-arm touch posing

  • Tap a part of the arm to select it.
  • Drag a part to pull it (IK). Dragging the hand moves the whole arm; dragging the forearm bends the elbow; dragging the upper arm swings the shoulder.
  • Long-press a part to show rotation rings for its joint, then drag a ring to rotate around that axis. Put the rings away with Done, by tapping the part again, or by tapping empty space.
  • One finger on empty space orbits. Two fingers pinch to zoom and drag to pan.
  • S Pen: hover highlights; holding the side button while dragging orbits.
  • Limits toggles anatomical joint limits. Hold hand: the hand keeps its angle in the world while you drag it.

What to react to: does selecting land on the part you meant? Is your finger hiding what you're posing? Do the rings feel controllable? Does the elbow go where you expect?
